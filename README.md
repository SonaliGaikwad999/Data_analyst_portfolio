## 📊 Projects

Click a project to expand its findings and recommendations.

<details>
<summary><b>🛍 Customer Behavior Dashboard</b> | Python, MySQL, Power BI, DAX</summary>

<br>

**Question:** Who buys, what they buy and how much they spend across 3,900 customers?

**Key numbers:** 233,081 total revenue · 59.76 average purchase · 3.75/5 average rating · 27% subscribers

**Findings**

- Clothing brings in 44.7% of revenue, followed by Accessories (31.8%).
- Average spend stays between 57 and 62 across every segment, so segment size matters more than spend.
- Subscribers spend and rate the same as non-subscribers.

**Recommendations**

- Grow the customer base in the top categories instead of pushing higher spend per customer.
- Review whether the subscription programme gives subscribers enough extra value.

[View repository](https://github.com/SonaliGaikwad999/customer_shopping_behaviour)

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📱 Mobile Sales Dashboard</b> | Power Query, Data Modelling, Power BI, DAX</summary>

<br>

**Question:** How do 3,835 transactions (Oct 2021 – Oct 2024) perform by brand, city and period?

**Key numbers:** 76.9 crore total sales · 19,150 units · 5 brands · 19 cities

**Findings**

- Delhi (26.5%) and Mumbai (16.5%) together make up 43% of sales.
- Brands are evenly split (Apple 21.0%, Xiaomi 18.7%).
- Full-year revenue is stable with a gentle decline of about 3%.
- 21.8% of sales carry a "Poor" rating.

**Recommendations**

- Protect the two core cities while testing growth in the other 17.
- Investigate the causes of poor ratings to lift customer satisfaction.

[View repository](https://github.com/SonaliGaikwad999/Mobile_sales_Analysis)

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>👥 HR Attrition Dashboard</b> | Power Query, Power BI, DAX</summary>

<br>

**Question:** Who is leaving among 1,470 employees, and what do they have in common?

**Key numbers:** 237 leavers · 16.1% attrition rate · average age 36.9 · average monthly income 6,503

**Findings**

- Overtime workers leave at 30.5% vs 10.4% for those who do not.
- Under-25s leave at 39.2%, and Sales Representatives at 39.8%.
- Leavers earned about 4,787 a month vs 6,833 for stayers.

**Recommendations**

- Review overtime workloads.
- Focus retention on early-career staff and Sales roles.
- Benchmark entry-level pay.

*These are correlations, not proven causes.*

[View repository](https://github.com/SonaliGaikwad999/HR-interactive-Dashboard)

</details>
